Our Mission

我们的使命

A loving Christian community meeting every Sabbath for family-friendly Bible study and worship, bringing people into a relationship with Jesus, with one another and with the community.

我们是一个充满爱的基督徒群体，每个安息日举行适合全家参加的查经与崇拜，帮助人与耶稣、与彼此、与社区建立爱的关系。

What to Expect

您可以期待什么

  • Attire: Come as you are — smart casual is common.
  • 着装：随意即可，整洁休闲装最常见。
  • Parking: Street parking around Jersey Rd; Strathfield station is a short walk. Please check local signs.
  • 停车：Jersey Rd 周边路边停车；斯特拉斯菲尔德火车站步行可达。请留意路牌限制。
  • Environment: Warm, family-friendly, bilingual-minded community. Bible Study 9:30 am, Divine Worship 10:40 am.
  • 环境：温馨、适合家庭。查经 9:30，崇拜 10:40。
